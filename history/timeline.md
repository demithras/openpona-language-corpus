# Condensed timeline

## 2026-09-09 to 2026-09-10 — Core42 exploration

Early 42-cell matrices, Thoth/decan structure and cross-domain semantic-kernel ideas were explored. The planetary column mnemonic (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) dates from this phase; since 2026-10-01 it is kept only as a memory aid in `LEARN_OPENPONA.md`, Lesson 1. These artifacts are valuable history but used a different token architecture.

## 2026-09-10 to 2026-09-11 — 36+6 freeze and `anu 1.0`

The language stabilized around 36 semantic tokens plus six structural operators. The project name became OpenPona and the version operator `anu` was adopted in naming.

## 2026-09-11 — held-out vocabulary attack

Omitted Toki Pona vocabulary was reconstructed compositionally. The test supported a relational/transformation kernel while exposing scalar/sensory weaknesses.

## 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-21 — addressing and runtime deepening

Primitives were treated as operations; two-or-more-token combinations as contextual concepts/addresses; nested context and stable machine binding were developed. The LINJA corpus consolidated `anu 1.1` and event/agent uses.

## 2026-09-28 — grammar and ontology-machine attacks

META priority, structural-position rules and explicit `pi` grouping were accepted. Generic grounding/authority/runtime hypotheses were attacked. OpenPona's role was narrowed away from being technically required by EOO.

## 2026-09-28 to 2026-09-29 — matrix reconstruction attacks

Single-header models were rejected. Semantic-intersection and external-coordinate models performed better. Weak coordinate reconstruction survived strong internal holdouts; exact lexical generation and unique optimality remain unproven.

## 2026-09-29 — new target

OpenPona is now a **candidate Ontology Language**, to be tested against a backend-neutral typed IR and a Palantir-class `Language × Engine × Toolchain` target rather than assumed as the EOO core.
