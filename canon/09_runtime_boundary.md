# 09 — Language/runtime boundary

**Status: CANON boundary; Ontology-Language role is RESEARCH.**

OpenPona may be used as:

- human/agent semantic notation;
- contextual addressing language;
- operational narrative/event notation;
- orchestration/control protocol;
- hypothesis notation;
- candidate surface syntax for a typed ontology IR.

It must not be assumed to be:

- the evidence store;
- the authorization engine;
- the policy engine;
- the transaction engine;
- a replacement for stable IDs;
- a complete type system until demonstrated.

The strongest current research question is whether OpenPona can serve as a **surface Ontology Language** that compiles losslessly into a backend-neutral typed IR while preserving the 42-token kernel. Failure of that hypothesis would not invalidate OpenPona as an agent/human operational language.
