# 01 — Definition and design contract

**Status: CANON**

OpenPona is a 42-token Executable Operational Language (EOL): a minimal operational language of agency and state transition whose resolved statements can be bound and executed by an external runtime (see `09_runtime_boundary.md`). Its 42-token kernel is deliberately bounded; domain richness should arise from composition, context and binding rather than uncontrolled vocabulary growth.

A useful working model is:

```text
surface tokens
→ semantic expressions
→ contextual bindings
→ typed/operational interpretation
→ optional execution by an external runtime
```

The language is optimized for relations, transformations, narrative state, operational direction and contextual addressing. An early, unpublished held-out vocabulary test (`research/heldout_vocabulary_experiment.md`) suggested it is weaker at scalar magnitude, sensory polarity and coordinate-like qualities. Those weaknesses are properties to acknowledge, not reasons to silently expand the kernel.

## Non-goals

OpenPona is not required to:

- replace natural language;
- encode every scalar value without external literals/data;
- act as an evidence database;
- decide authorization merely from syntax;
- make domain-expert judgment automatically;
- be the storage model of an executable ontology;
- prove mystical/planetary correspondences as empirical truth.

The 6 × 7 narrative/planetary organization is part mnemonic, part structural design. Its operational value is testable; occult correspondence is not used as evidence.
