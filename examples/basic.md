# Basic OpenPona examples

These examples illustrate grammar and operational distinctions. They are not a claim that every English gloss is uniquely recoverable from surface text without context.

## Predication

```text
ilo sitelen li awen
```

Possible render in a context where `ilo sitelen` resolves to a logging tool:

> The writing/logging tool persists/remains available.

## Directed relation

```text
jan pali li pana e sitelen
```

A work-agent emits/provides a representation/document.

## Context

```text
ma pali la jan li lukin e ijo
```

Within the work/project scope, an agent inspects an entity.

## Provenance

```text
sona ni li kama tan kute
```

The bound knowledge/model came from a received signal.

## Alternative

```text
nasin open anu nasin awen
```

An explicit branch between an opening path and a retaining/continuing path.

## Unknown

```text
seme li tan e ni
```

The source/cause of the bound referent is unresolved. A runtime should preserve the unresolved state rather than fill it heuristically.
